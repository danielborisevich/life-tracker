      Study methods

      Active recall

      Close the notes and write out what you remember, then check. Retrieval builds memory far more than rereading.

      Spaced repetition

      Review cards just before you forget them. The flashcards here push a card to a longer gap (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days) each time you get it right.

      Feynman technique

      Explain a concept in plain words as if teaching a friend. Wherever you stall is the gap to study next.

      Interleaving

      Mix problem types in one session (e.g. enzyme kinetics, then buffers, then kinetics again) instead of blocking one type.

      Blurting

      After a focus block, spend the break's first two minutes dumping everything you just learned onto paper.

          Apple Calendar & Canvas

          Paste a calendar's share link and its events show up here. They refresh every time you open the app.

            How to get your Apple Calendar link
            1. On your iPhone, open Calendar and tap Calendars at the bottom.
            2. Tap ⓘ next to the calendar you want (for example your class schedule).
            3. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link…, then Copy.
            4. Paste it above and tap Add.
            5. Canvas: open Calendar on Canvas, click Calendar Feed at the bottom right, and copy that link.

            Anyone who has a public link can see that calendar, so only share calendars you're comfortable with.
